# Third-party notices

## BossmodReborn / BossMod

Reference for the retained POTD room coordinates, trap observations, transformation mechanics and minimal ground-input seam (the embedded engine, Clipper2, HashCode and xxHash implementations were removed):
`FFXIV-CombatReborn/BossmodReborn@fb9bb56cc06735635e0f25f028ef5df875472309`.

POTD floor-set 6-20 room centers were extended from the existing local
BossmodReborn source at revision `839861060` (LoadedFloors.cs). Earlier
DDuck room centers remain unchanged. No BMR assembly or engine is embedded.
HoH/EO room centers for their ten floor sets use the two ordinary layouts in
`LoadedFloors.cs` at `8e15ce90b841eddc9a0d606aae8579272582e8e8`.
The initialization mapping follows `WorldStateGameSync.cs` at that revision;
Hall of Fallacies and PT layouts are not inferred from those coordinates.
The existing `KnownPotdTraps.cs` table now includes all forty territories from
`Global/DeepDungeon/TrapData.cs` at tag `7.5.6.14` (`8e15ce90`), plus the eight
revealed trap object IDs from `AutoClear.cs`. The full table has 14,176 historical
Palace Pal observations covering POTD, HoH and EO; PT positions are not inferred.
The original five POTD territory tables were preserved unchanged. Historical
points guide path preference; native revealed traps remain actual exclusions.
Completed floor-60/70/80 boss staging uses the arena center documented in
`SharedBounds.cs` at that same revision; passage interaction still uses the
locally observed native object.
Floor-100 completed-passage staging uses an interior point of the arena polygon
in `DD100NybethObdilord.cs`, corroborated by local passage visibility on that floor.
The floor-180 manual burst window follows `DD180Dendainsonne.ManualBurst`
at the same revision; DDuck's existing action handoff handles that window.
Floor-90 Grey Bomb and floor-100 Corse targeting follow the encounter add
components in `DD90TheGodmother.cs` and `DD100NybethObdilord.cs` at that revision.
The same targeting review includes `DD120Kirtimukha.cs`, `DD140AhPuch.cs`
and `DD190TheGodfather.cs` for Hornet, Follower and Remedy Bomb adds.
Floor-150 role-specific boss/zombie priorities follow `DD150Tisiphone.BossAdds`
at the same revision; BMR continues to own its zombie avoidance mechanics.

BossmodReborn preserves the BossMod lineage of Andrew Gilewsky and the
Deep Dungeon/autorotation work associated with Xan/Veyn, with later work by the
Combat Reborn team and contributors credited in the upstream source, including
Malediktus and Akechi. CarnifexOptimus authored the selected snapshot commit.
Per-file upstream attribution and project history remain the authoritative
module-level provenance records.
